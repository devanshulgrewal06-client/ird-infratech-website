OUR STORY

Infrastructure that stands on strong foundations.

IRD Infratech Private Limited was established in 2024 in Modinagar, Ghaziabad, Uttar Pradesh. We introduce ourselves as infrastructure developers, providing civil construction services and contributing to the development of roads, warehouses, boundary walls, protection walls and water-supply connections.

Under civil construction, our work includes roads, landscaping and beautification, traffic management, rainwater harvesting, drainage, designing, fabrication and installation of infrastructure-related services.
